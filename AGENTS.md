## Keeping ProjectExperience.md current

`ProjectExperience.md` at the repo root is a living, self-contained summary of this project and the skills it demonstrates. Its audience is not this repo's developers — it gets pasted into AI sessions (resume building, cover letters, interview prep), so it must stand alone without repo access, stay strictly factual (every claim interview-defensible), and describe skills in resume-ready language: action verb, what was built, scale or outcome. It has two lenses that both matter: skills demonstrated (shipped and running) and skills in development (actively being explored). Jargon and acronyms are fine — the consuming AI translates for the audience.

Update it at milestones, not on every change. Milestones: an epic or phase ships, a new technology or skill area enters the project, a metric worth citing changes materially (scale, accuracy, uptime, dataset size), or a section no longer reflects reality. Routine bug fixes, refactors, and incremental feature work do not qualify. When closing out work that plausibly crosses that bar, check whether `ProjectExperience.md` needs a touch-up and include the edit in the same PR — and refresh its "Last updated" date whenever you do.
